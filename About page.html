About Our Website and Store

Everything you need to know about how and why this site was built.

Who Created This Website

This website was designed and developed by Lesedi Reabetsoe Tsutsubi (Student Number: ST10519854), for the Web Development module (WEDE5020), Group 2, at Rosebank International (The IIE).

Who This Website Is For

Revive Thrift Co. is aimed at students and young professionals in Braamfontein, Johannesburg, who are looking for affordable, quality second-hand and vintage fashion. The site gives them an easy way to browse what's in stock, find the store, and get in touch before visiting.

Why This Website Was Created

Braamfontein previously had a well-known thrift store that has since closed permanently, leaving a gap in the area's second-hand fashion scene. This project imagines a new thrift business, Revive Thrift Co., stepping into that space — and gives it the kind of dedicated online presence the old store never had, so customers aren't relying on an outdated Facebook page alone.

Fun Facts About the Project

  • The colour palette (mustard, forest green, and cream) was chosen deliberately to evoke a warm, vintage feel.
  • Revive Thrift Co. is a fictional business, loosely inspired by a real Braamfontein thrift store that has since closed.
  • The site was planned first as a full written proposal before a single line of code was written.
  • Everything on this page — layout, colours, and content — was built from scratch as part of a first-year web development module.
